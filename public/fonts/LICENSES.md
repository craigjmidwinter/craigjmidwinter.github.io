# Site fonts

The site's two typefaces are served from this site (self-hosted with next/font; card e2d93c).
Both are free software under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their licence texts are next to this file.

| Font | Weights | Licence | Upstream |
|---|---|---|---|
| JetBrains Mono | 400, 500, 700 (latin) | SIL OFL 1.1, [OFL-JetBrainsMono.txt](OFL-JetBrainsMono.txt) | https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono |
| Space Grotesk | 400, 500, 700 (latin) | SIL OFL 1.1, [OFL-SpaceGrotesk.txt](OFL-SpaceGrotesk.txt) | https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk |

The font files are the Google Fonts builds, which next/font downloads when the site is built
(Next 15.1.6) and serves from `/_next/static/media/`. They are not modified.
